## שאלות ותשובות

### מהו Must makes the action required?

The sign-in instruction is stronger than a suggestion. 'Visitors must sign their names' has must + base verb. The rule requires signing. 'Visitors must signs' wrongly adds an ending after must. The correct form is sign for all subjects: I must sign; she must sign; they must sign. The source identifies who the rule concerns. It does not create a general rule about unrelated places.

### מהו Mustn't forbids; it does not make an action optional?

Mustn't means must not. 'Visitors mustn't enter the storage room without a helper' prohibits that particular kind of entry. It does not say visitors are free to enter alone. The condition without a helper is important. An accurate shorter explanation is 'A helper is required for storage-room entry'. Removing the condition and saying 'Nobody may enter the room' would also change the rule.

### Why is 'You mustn't take a programme' an inaccurate explanation of 'A programme is optional'?

An optional programme allows visitors to choose whether to take one. 'You mustn't take a programme' prohibits the action. The paraphrase therefore changes permission into an instruction against taking it; it does not preserve the source's meaning.

## How do permission and prohibition differ?

A notice can tell a visitor what is required, what is forbidden, and what is allowed. Those meanings are different. Changing one small word in an explanation can turn an optional action into an obligation or make a forbidden action sound acceptable.

עזרה בעברית

בהודעה יכולים להופיע חובה, איסור ורשות באותו קטע. החלפה של מילה אחת עלולה להפוך בחירה לחובה, ולכן צריך להבין את סוג המסר לפני שמסכמים אותו.

## Words in a rule

An obligation is something a person is required to do. A prohibition forbids an action. To permit is to allow it. Optional means available as a choice rather than required. The question 'Is it permitted?' concerns permission in this setting, not whether a person has the physical ability to do it.

עזרה בעברית

חובה אומרת שצריך לבצע פעולה; איסור אומר שאין לבצע אותה. פעולה שהיא רשות או בחירה אינה חובה, ורשות גם אינה זהה ליכולת גופנית.

## Original permission exchange

Visitor: Can I use the blue table? Helper: Yes, visitors can use it during opening hours. Visitor: Can I put my bag on the red table instead? Helper: No, you can't. That table holds the displays. Visitor: Must I take a printed card? Helper: No. Taking a card is optional. The information is also on the wall. Visitor: Then I will read the wall notice. Helper: That is fine. Please sign in first.

עזרה בעברית

השאלות בודקות רשות וחובה, והתשובות מפרטות מה מותר, מה אסור ומה נתון לבחירה. בסוף מזכירים שהרישום קודם לשימוש במקום.

## Not required and forbidden

Optional card

'You don't have to take a card' is another way to explain 'Taking a card is optional'. A visitor may choose either.

Forbidden action

'You mustn't enter alone' rules out entering without the stated helper.

These expressions cannot replace each other. Not required leaves a choice; forbidden removes that choice.

עזרה בעברית

״לא חייבים״ משאיר אפשרות לבחור, ואילו ״אסור״ שולל את הפעולה. לכן don't have to ו-mustn't אינם תרגומים חלופיים לאותו מסר.

## Four paraphrases of the notice

1. 'Sign in before using the club.' This preserves the obligation. 2. 'Visitors can use the blue tables during opening hours.' This preserves the permission and time condition. 3. 'Every visitor must take a printed card.' This changes an optional action into an obligation. 4. 'Storage-room entry without a helper is optional.' This changes a prohibition into a choice. The accurate paraphrases preserve the strength and boundaries of each rule.

עזרה בעברית

בניסוח מחדש צריך לשמור על סוג הכלל ועל גבולותיו. שני המשפטים האחרונים מדגימים שינוי במשמעות: הפיכת בחירה לחובה והפיכת איסור לבחירה.

## A second context: a fictional exhibition

'Guests must keep the entrance clear. They can leave coats on the stand. They mustn't place boxes in the doorway. A programme is optional.' The coat stand gives a permitted location, but it does not require every guest to bring a coat. The box rule protects the named doorway; it does not prohibit every box in every part of the building.

עזרה בעברית

רשות להניח מעיל במתקן אינה מחייבת להביא מעיל. האיסור על ארגזים מתייחס לפתח הדלת המסוים, ולא לכל הבניין.

## Why is 'You mustn't take a programme' an inaccurate explanation of 'A programme is optional'?

An optional programme allows visitors to choose whether to take one. 'You mustn't take a programme' prohibits the action. The paraphrase therefore changes permission into an instruction against taking it; it does not preserve the source's meaning.

## Preserving a condition

A concise relay should retain the person, action, force, and important condition. 'Visitors can use the blue tables during opening hours' keeps all four. 'Anyone can use everything whenever they like' changes the people, objects, and time. Shortening is useful only when it preserves the information a reader needs.

עזרה בעברית

סיכום קצר צריך לשמור על ארבעה פרטים: למי הכלל מיועד, איזו פעולה הוא מתאר, מה תוקפו ובאילו תנאים הוא חל. קיצור שמשנה אנשים, חפצים או זמנים משנה גם את הכלל.
